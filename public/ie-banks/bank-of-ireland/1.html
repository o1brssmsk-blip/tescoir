Choose login method

If you do your banking through a browser, and use your mobile phone when logging in, you must create a 6-digit passcode as strong security for that device. This new passcode should be used instead of your 365 PIN to log in. If you normally log in with a Physical Security Key and do not use the mobile app for banking, you should log in as usual using your 365 PIN.
